Guba is one of the most colourful and green cities in Azerbaijan, located on the northeastern slopes of the Greater Caucasus just two hours from Baku. People come for century-old apple orchards, famous Guba carpets, cool canyons and the unique Red Settlement — the last compact community of Mountain Jews in the world outside Israel and the USA.

## Getting There from Baku

Distance 168 km on a modern highway.

**Bus** — cheapest option. Regular departures from the International Bus Terminal every 30–60 minutes. Travel time 2.5–3 hours, price 5 AZN one way.

**Shared taxi** — seat from "20 January" metro or bus terminal — 10–15 AZN per person. Private Bolt/Uber one way — 65–85 AZN.

## What to See

**Red Settlement (Qırmızı Qəsəbə)** — Jewish village connected to Guba by an arch bridge over the Gudyalchay river. Ancient synagogues alongside luxurious modern villas. Unique Museum of Mountain Jews. Walk for free, museum — 10 AZN.

**Arch Bridge (Tağlı körpü)** — 19th-century engineering masterpiece, the only surviving one of seven. Pedestrian zone connecting two cultures. Free entry.

**Gechresh Forest and Tengealti Canyon** — ancient relic forest 15 minutes from Guba where tree canopies form a living green tunnel. Further on — a deep canyon where the river is wedged between sheer cliffs. Free access, riverside lunch at local restaurants — 20–35 AZN.

**Guba Carpet Factory** — live demonstration of handwoven famous Caucasian carpets. Entry 5–10 AZN. Small handmade carpet from 250–300 AZN.

## What to Try

**Guba pakhlava** — made from thin rice noodles (rishta) with walnuts and spices. Less sweet and very delicate. Price 1.5–2.5 AZN per piece at local confectioneries.

**Tykhma** — local sweet pie with spiced nut filling.

**Forest shashlik** — lamb from tandoor with local herbs.
