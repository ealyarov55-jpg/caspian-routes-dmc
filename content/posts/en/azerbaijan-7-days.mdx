## Days 1–2: Baku — The Windy Capital

### Key Highlights

- **Icheri Sheher (Old City):** Maiden Tower (15 AZN) and the Palace of the Shirvanshahs (15 AZN). Don't miss Ali Shamsi's art studio
- **Baku Boulevard:** A boat trip in the bay (10 AZN) offers the best panoramic view of the skyline
- **Heydar Aliyev Center:** Museum entry 15 AZN, but the exterior is a free playground for photographers

> 🔗 **Hotels in Baku:** [see options](https://ostrovok.tpk.ro/DDho2QGw)

## Day 3: Gobustan — Fire & Mud

### Logistics (Crucial)

- **Public Transport:** Do NOT go to "Avtovagzal". Take Bus #125 from "28 May" or "Azneft" to 20th Area (20-ci sahə), then transfer to Bus #195. Total: 1.20 AZN
- **Taxi:** Round trip with waiting driver — 60–70 AZN
- **Attractions:** Petroglyphs (15 AZN) + New Mud Volcano Tourism Complex (~15 AZN)

> 🔗 **Guided Gobustan tour from Baku:** [see options](https://www.getyourguide.com/baku-l1408/?partner_id=YNRQ0A3)

## Day 4: Sheki — The Heart of the Silk Road

### Getting There

- **Train:** Fast Stadler train ~6 hours, overnight sleeper 8–9 hours. Price: 15–25 AZN
- **Shared Taxi:** From Avtovagzal, 25 AZN per seat, ~4.5 hours

### Must See

- **Sheki Khan's Palace** — 15 AZN
- **Kish Village** — ancient Albanian Church (10 AZN), 10 mins by taxi from Sheki center. Mystical and ancient atmosphere

> 🔗 **Hotels in Sheki:** [see options](https://ostrovok.tpk.ro/DDho2QGw)

---

## Day 5: Gabala — The Caucasian Alps

### Logistics

From Sheki to Gabala — 1.5 hours by taxi (~25 AZN)

### What to Do

- **Tufandag Resort:** Cable car — 30 AZN for a full-line pass
- **Nohur Lake:** Altitude ~700m, beautiful though crowded. Boat ride 10–15 AZN
- **7 Beauties Waterfall:** Vandam village, 12 km from Gabala center. Taxi 7–10 AZN

> 🔗 **Hotels in Gabala:** [see options](https://ostrovok.tpk.ro/DDho2QGw)

---

## Day 6: Lahij — The Village of Copper

### Logistics

From Gabala to Ismayilli, then taxi up the mountain serpentines to Lahij (~25 AZN)

Walk the cobblestone streets, listen to the rhythmic hammering of coppersmiths, buy mountain spices. It's like the 18th century never ended.

---

## Day 7: Baku — The Final Chord

- **Carpet Museum** — entry 10 AZN. The most photogenic building on the Boulevard
- **Shopping:** Saffron, tea, pomegranate wine. Skip the tourist shops — head to Yashil Bazar (Green Market) for the best prices
