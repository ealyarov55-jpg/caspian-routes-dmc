Children are treated like royalty here. Museums are free for kids under 6, and downtown Baku is highly stroller-friendly. Don't expect free restaurant meals — portions are massive, so ordering one dish for two kids is the standard practice.

---

## Best Spots for Children in Baku

### Baku Zoo

A completely renovated modern eco-park with spacious enclosures and well-cared-for animals.

- **Price:** Adults — 5 AZN, Kids — 2 AZN

### Baku Boulevard & Baku Eye

The main seafront promenade packed with carousels and playgrounds. Don't miss the giant Ferris wheel.

- **Price:** Rides — 3–5 AZN, Ferris wheel — 10 AZN

### Kinderland (Deniz Mall)

A massive indoor edutainment city where kids can roleplay as AZAL pilots, firefighters or doctors.

- **Price:** Kids — 35–45 AZN (all-day pass), Adults — 10 AZN

### Little Venice

A scenic gondola ride along the artificial water canals on the Boulevard.

- **Price:** 10 AZN per passenger (kids under 3 ride free)

## Day Trips with Kids

### Gobustan & Mud Volcanoes

Ancient rock art and bubbling mud structures. The mud is cold and safe — but pack a change of clothes and wet wipes.

### Gabala (Gabaland & Tufandag)

Gabaland amusement park (entry 5 AZN, rides priced separately) and mountain cable cars. Great for kids aged 5+.

### Shamakhi (Safari Park & Observatory)

Skip the ruins — take your kids to the Safari Park to spot wild deer, or visit the Astrophysical Observatory at night to look at planets through a giant telescope. Best for kids 8+.

## Practical Tips

- **Food:** Azerbaijani food is flavorful but never spicy. Kids love cheese qutabs, chicken kebabs and saffron rice. Dushbara soup (micro-dumplings) is a lifesaver for toddlers
- **Transport:** Always select the «Kids» tariff in Bolt or Uber AZ — only this guarantees a child car seat
- **Medical care:** Pediatrician at top private clinics (Bona Dea, Leyla Medical) — 60–80 AZN. Pharmacies open 24/7
