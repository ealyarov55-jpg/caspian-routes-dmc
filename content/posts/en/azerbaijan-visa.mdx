## Visa-Free Entry (Up to 90 Days)

Citizens of the following countries do not need a visa. A passport valid for at least 6 months from the date of entry is mandatory:

- Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova, Tajikistan
- Turkey — up to 90 days
- Georgia — up to 90 days
- Ukraine

## ASAN E-Visa (90+ Countries)

For citizens of the EU, USA, Canada, Gulf countries, China and others via [evisa.gov.az](https://evisa.gov.az).

- **Standard:** Issued in 3 business days — $26
- **Urgent:** Issued in 3 hours, 24/7 — $60
- **Duration:** Valid for 90 days, allows a single stay of up to 30 days

## The 15-Day Registration Rule

The most overlooked rule for tourists. If staying in Azerbaijan for more than 15 days, you must register with the State Migration Service.

- **Hotels:** Register you automatically at check-in
- **Airbnb/private rentals:** The host must register you via [migration.gov.az](https://migration.gov.az)
- **Cost:** Free
- **Penalty:** 300–400 AZN fine + potential deportation and entry ban

---

## Special Restrictions

- **Armenian heritage:** Travelers of Armenian descent or with Armenian surnames may face additional screening or denial of entry at border control
- **Nagorno-Karabakh:** Anyone who visited the Karabakh region before 2020 without Azerbaijani permission is permanently blacklisted and denied entry
