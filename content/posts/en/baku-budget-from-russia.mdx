## Flight Moscow — Baku

Many direct flights available (Aeroflot, AZAL, Utair, S7). Price depends on how early you book.

**Prices (round trip, May 2026):**
- Basic (no luggage): 11,000–16,000 ₽
- With luggage: 18,000–25,000 ₽
- High demand (holidays): from 35,000 ₽

## Visa and Documents

- **Visa:** not required (up to 90 days)
- **Documents:** international passport only. Must be valid for at least 6 months at entry
- **Registration:** if staying more than 15 days — your hotel is required to register you with migration services

## Food (Daily Budget)

| Option | Price (AZN) | What's included |
|--------|-------------|-----------------|
| Street food | 4–7 AZN | Doner or 2 qutabs + ayran |
| Business lunch | 12–18 AZN | Soup, main, salad, tea |
| Restaurant dinner | 35–60 AZN | Saj or kebab, appetizers, wine |

**Minimum food budget:** 30 AZN (~$18) per day per person without extras.

## Transport and Tickets — 2026 Reality

- **Metro and bus:** 0.50 AZN per ride. BakiKart required (2 AZN)
- **Taxi (Bolt/Uber):** city center — 4–8 AZN. Airport — 12–18 AZN
- **Museum tickets:** standard price for foreigners at top venues (Maiden Tower, Shirvanshahs, Heydar Aliyev Center) — 15 AZN each

- Russian Visa/Mastercard cards still don't work
- **What to bring:** cash in USD, EUR or RUB
- **Where to exchange:** any bank or official exchange office (many on Torgovaya Street)
