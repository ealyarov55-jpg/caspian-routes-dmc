Gabala is Shahdag's main competitor. In winter it's not just «beautiful» — it's alive, and prices bite almost as hard as the mountain frost.

---

## How to Get There from Baku

**Bus / Minibus:** only from the International Bus Terminal (metro «Avtovogzal»).
- Journey time: 3.5–4 hours
- Price: 12–15 AZN

**Taxi:**
- Shared taxi (per seat): 20–25 AZN
- Private booking: 100–130 AZN per car. Best option for a group of four

---

## Tufandag Ski Resort

Tufandag sits right at the edge of the city. In 2026 prices are firmly in the premium segment.

- **Day ski pass:** 40–50 AZN
- **Sightseeing lift (no skiing):** 30 AZN
- **Equipment rental (skis/snowboard + boots):** 30–40 AZN per day
- **Instructor:** from 50 AZN per hour

## Other Activities in Gabala

- **Nohur Lake (Nohur Gölü):** often freezes over in winter. Horse riding around the lake — 15–20 AZN
- **Gabaland:** partially open in winter, good for a walk and photos. Entry: 5 AZN
- **Gabala Shooting Club:** skeet shooting from 30 AZN per round

---

## Food: Warming Up Properly

- **Dash-arasi (meat between stones)** — local speciality
- **Saj** — large pan with meat and vegetables on coals. For two: ~40–50 AZN
- **Tea set** with walnut or white cherry jam — 15–20 AZN
