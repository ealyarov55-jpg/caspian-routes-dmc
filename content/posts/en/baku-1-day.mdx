One day in Baku is a sprint. Skip the buses, use taxi apps and start as early as possible.

---

## Morning: Icherisheher (09:00 — 12:00)

The Old City is the heart of Baku. Arrive at opening — by noon tourist groups fill the narrow streets.

- **Maiden Tower** — 15 AZN for foreigners. Open daily 09:00–18:00
- **Palace of the Shirvanshahs** — 15 AZN. The complex is large, allow at least an hour
- **Museum of Miniature Books** — free. Closed Mondays and Thursdays

**Breakfast:** skip fancy places. Get qutabs (with greens, meat or pumpkin) from tandoor shops. Price: 2–4 AZN. Add tea in an armud glass.

---

## Afternoon: Boulevard and Little Venice (12:00 — 16:00)

Exit through the southern gates to the seafront. The boulevard stretches over 15 km — you won't cover it on foot.

- **Electric scooter rental** — 8–12 AZN/hour. Faster and more practical than walking
- **Little Venice** — gondola ride 10 AZN. Worth it for photos

**Lunch:**
- Budget (25 AZN): «Fisincan» or «Anadolu»
- Mid-range (50+ AZN): «Dolma» at Fountain Square. Try the Shah-plov

---

## Evening: Highland Park and Flame Towers (16:00 — 20:00)

The best finale for the day. Here you see the city transition from antiquity to futurism.

- **Funicular** (1 AZN) — unreliable, often under maintenance. Call Bolt directly to Flame Towers (~3–4 AZN)
- **Light show** starts right after sunset. In May that's around 20:00

**Dinner:** head back to Fountain Square (Torgovaya). In the evening this is the highest concentration of life in the city.

---

## Practical Info — May 2026

**Transport:** metro and bus — 0.50 AZN. BakiKart (2 AZN) shared between group. Only Bolt or Uber AZ — never negotiate with street drivers, you'll overpay 3x.

**Money:** bring cash. Exchange at official offices on Torgovaya Street. Rate: 1 AZN ≈ $0.59.

**Heat warning:** in May temperatures can reach +28°C. Bring water and a hat.

**Watch out:** don't accept «free» tours from strangers in the Old City.
