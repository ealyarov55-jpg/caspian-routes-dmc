Sheki is a portal to the 18th century. Different air, different pace of life and completely different cuisine. Distance from Baku — 300 km.

---

## How to Get There from Baku

**Bus / Minibus:** departs from the International Bus Terminal (metro «Avtovogzal»).
- Journey time: 5.5–6 hours
- Price: 15 AZN
- Tip: take the first departure at 07:00 to arrive by lunchtime

**Train:** overnight service Baku — Balakan (Sheki stop).
- Price: compartment ~20–25 AZN
- Arrives 06:30 — perfect to start the day early

**Shared Taxi:** cars wait at the bus terminal.
- Price: 25–30 AZN per seat
- Journey: 4–4.5 hours

---

## Itinerary: What to Fit In

### Morning: Palace and Shebeke (10:00 — 12:30)

- **Palace of the Sheki Khans** — UNESCO gem. Entry for foreigners: 15 AZN. Photography inside is officially prohibited
- **Shebeke workshops** — right behind the palace. Watch craftsmen assemble stained glass without glue or nails. Free

### Lunch: Piti Time (13:00 — 14:30)

Sheki piti is not just soup — it's a ritual served as two dishes in one clay pot.

- **Where to eat:** restaurant «Gagarin» or inside the «Upper Caravanserai»
- **Price:** 12–18 AZN. Very filling — you'll want to nap after, but there's no time

### Afternoon: History and Kish (14:30 — 17:00)

- **Upper Caravanserai** — a medieval hotel. Walk into the courtyard — free and very atmospheric
- **Kish village** — 5 km from Sheki, taxi 5–7 AZN. Ancient Albanian church. Entry: 10 AZN. One of the most mystical places in the region

---

## What to Bring Back from Sheki

- **Sheki halva** — buy only fresh (brand «Aliahmed» is the classic). Price: 7–10 AZN per box
- **Silk scarves (Kelagayi)** — look in shops near the palace. Real silk from 40 AZN
