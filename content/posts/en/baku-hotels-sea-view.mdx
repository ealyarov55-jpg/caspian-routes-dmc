A Caspian sunset from your hotel window is the main tourist trap in Baku. The city is actively building up the waterfront, so what looked like sea in Booking photos may turn out to be a concrete skeleton of a new skyscraper.

---

## Three Rules for Choosing «Sea View» in 2026

1. **Avoid low floors:** on Neftchilar Avenue, views from below the 5th floor are often blocked by century-old Boulevard trees or pavilions
2. **Side matters:** Fairmont has «City View» rooms — cheaper but sea is only visible at an angle. Insist on «Full Sea View»
3. **Port Baku construction:** active construction near the sea terminal. If the hotel is in that area — check for cranes outside the window

---

## Hotels with Guaranteed Panoramic Views

### Luxury ($250+ per night)

- **Fairmont Baku** — inside the Flame Towers. Best view in the city due to height. You can see everything from the port to Shikhovo
- **JW Marriott Absheron** — perfect location right on the Boulevard. «Executive» category rooms guarantee a straight horizon
- **Four Seasons Baku** — view over «European» Baku and the Boulevard. Very prestigious
- **Dinamo Hotel** — boutique luxury. Sea is close, view is more intimate

### Mid-Range ($120–220 per night)

Prices have risen — mid-range now starts from $120.

- **Hilton Baku** — rotating rooftop bar (360 Bar) gives the best view even if you're not a guest
- **Park Inn by Radisson** — solid option right opposite the Boulevard. View of the Ferris wheel and sea

> 🔗 **Check availability at sea-view hotels:** [see options](https://ostrovok.tpk.ro/DDho2QGw)

### Budget Segment ($70–100 per night)

Honest answer: budget hotels with sea views don't exist. Below $70 it's either a «side» view or a hotel in Shikhovo near industrial zones.

**Your plan:** stay near «Sahil» or «28 May» metro. 10 minutes walk to the sea — spend the savings on dinner at a terrace restaurant.

---

## Beach Options (Absheron 2026)

Swimming in the city bay is still not allowed.

- **Sea Breeze (Nardaran)** — most fashionable cluster. Bolt from center ~20–25 AZN
- **Amburan (Bilgah)** — classic with excellent service
- **Shikhovo** — closest to city (15 min), but water is traditionally dirtier due to proximity to oil platforms
