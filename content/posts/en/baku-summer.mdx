Summer in Baku means white heat (+35–42°C). The city follows a siesta rule: between 13:00 and 17:00 there is nothing to do outdoors.

---

## Season Overview 2026

- **Heat:** humidity rises in July-August. Look for places marked «AC»
- **Evening peak:** the city comes alive after 19:00. The Boulevard is packed until 02:00
- **Wind:** the «Khazri» (north wind) brings relief but can kick up sand on beaches

---

## Morning: Before the Heat (08:00 — 12:00)

### Icherisheher

The Old City is the only district where narrow streets create natural shade.

- **Maiden Tower** — 15 AZN. Go right at opening (09:00) before the stone heats up

**Breakfast:** order «Kyukyu» (herb omelette) or a classic Azerbaijani breakfast with shor cheese and honey.

---

## Afternoon: Survival Plan (12:00 — 17:00)

Indoors only — powerful air conditioning required.

- **Heydar Aliyev Center** — 15 AZN. Huge cool white space, perfect for photos and museum
- **Carpet Museum** — 15 AZN. Unusual building shaped like a rolled carpet on the Boulevard. Great AC inside

**Seasonal lunch menu:**
- **Ovdukh** — Azerbaijani cold soup on matsoni. Best relief from the heat
- **Watermelon with cheese (Peynir-Qarpız)** — sounds odd but this is the main summer dessert in Baku

## Evening: Sea and Promenades (17:00 — 22:00)

### Absheron Beaches

Swimming in the city bay is not allowed. Take Bolt taxi (~10–15 AZN):

- **Bilgah / Sea Breeze** — premium beaches, clean water, entry from 25–40 AZN
- **Mardakan / Shuvelan** — more affordable, many family water parks
- **Nardaran** — clean sea, more conservative atmosphere

### Seaside Boulevard

Everything operates in the evening:
- Bicycle rental — 10 AZN/hour
- Boat ride around the bay — 10 AZN (sea breeze is the only relief in the evening)

---

## Summer 2026 Checklist

- **Water:** buy bottled only (Badamli/Sirab). Tap water is not for drinking
- **Clothing:** natural linen or cotton
- **SPF:** minimum 50+. Baku sun burns in 15 minutes
