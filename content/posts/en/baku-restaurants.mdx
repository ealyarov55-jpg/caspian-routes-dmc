## Street Food & Teahouses (10–25 AZN per person)

- **Qutabs** — thin stuffed pancakes. In Icheri Sheher (Old City), expect 3–5 AZN per piece. Look for small tandir bakeries inside the fortress walls for the most authentic taste
- **Dushbara** — tiny meat dumplings in broth. A portion in tourist-friendly spots costs 10–12 AZN
- **Tea & Pakhlava** — a simple tea for 1.5 AZN is no longer a reality in the center. A standard tea set (pot, jams, pakhlava, nuts) costs 20–30 AZN. A single armudu glass of tea — 2–3 AZN

## Mid-Range (35–60 AZN per person)

This is where you pay for atmosphere and consistent quality.

- **Firuze** — cozy basement right on Fountain Square. Incredible decor with antique carpets
- **Nergiz** — neighbour to Firuze and a local classic. Famous for traditional Saj and Dolma
- **Qadim Baki (Old Baku)** — inside the Old City. Feels like visiting a wealthy 18th-century Baku home
- **Dolma** — popular spot on Nizami (Torgovaya) Street. Large portions and a huge variety of national dishes without unnecessary luxury markups

## Fine Dining (80–180 AZN per person)

Premium service, unique presentation, high-end ingredients.

- **Shirvanshah Museum Restaurant** — literally a museum. You dine in halls filled with genuine antiques. Their Shah Plov presentation is a theatrical event
- **Sumakh** — a modern take on tradition. Sleek interior, elite crowd, flawless execution of Azerbaijani classics
- **Mugham Club** — housed in a medieval caravanserai in the Old City. Often features live Mugham folk music in the evenings. Very atmospheric

---

## Must-Try Dishes

- **Shah Plov** — saffron rice baked inside a crispy crust. Usually requires a 2-hour pre-order, though some places offer single portions
- **Lyulya Kebab** — minced meat grilled on skewers, served with lavash and onions
- **Dovga** — refreshing cold yogurt soup with greens. Essential for the May heat
- **Narsharab** — pomegranate sauce. Pour it on everything — from fish to grilled meats
- **Tea in Armudu** — served with lump sugar and lemon. The heart of Azerbaijani hospitality
