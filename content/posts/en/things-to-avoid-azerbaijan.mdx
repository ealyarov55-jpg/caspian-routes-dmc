## Unauthorized Entry to Liberated Territories

Visiting regions like Shusha or Aghdam without a government permit via the "Yolumuz Qarabağa" portal or an official tour is strictly prohibited.

**Consequence:** Immediate arrest and deportation.

---

## Strategic Photography

Baku is incredibly photogenic, but some subjects will get you in serious trouble.

- **Prohibited:** Military installations, government buildings, security personnel and equipment
- **Metro:** Amateur photography is fine, but professional gear (tripods, flash equipment) requires a permit

---

## Drones (UAVs)

Bringing a drone into Azerbaijan without a license from the Ministry of Digital Development is forbidden.

Reality: your drone will most likely be seized by customs at Heydar Aliyev Airport and held until your departure.

## Insulting State Symbols

Publicly disrespecting the Azerbaijani flag, national anthem, or the President is a criminal offense under Azerbaijani law. This applies to social media posts made while in the country.

---

## Social Etiquette

- **The "OK" gesture 👌** — can be interpreted as a vulgar insult in Azerbaijan. Use thumbs up instead
- **Public displays of affection:** Baku is a liberal city, but excessive kissing or hugging near mosques and religious sites is strongly frowned upon
- **Littering:** Fines for littering in parks and on the Boulevard are actively enforced in 2026
