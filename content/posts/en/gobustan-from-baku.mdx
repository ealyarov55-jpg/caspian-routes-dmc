Gobustan is a must-visit. But going there unprepared means overpaying taxi drivers or getting stuck in dust. In 2026 the location has become more civilized — and more expensive.

---

## What to See

- **Interactive museum and petroglyphs** — entry 15 AZN for foreigners. Modern museum followed by a walk among the rocks
- **Mud volcanoes** — a new tourist complex has opened. This is no longer a «wild field» but a civilized zone with roads and infrastructure

---

## How to Get There

### Option 1: Public Transport (Budget)

- Bus №125 from «28 May» metro or «Azneft» stop to «Bina» / «Sadarak» (Lokbatan) — 0.50 AZN
- Transfer to bus №195 to Gobustan village — 0.50 AZN
- From the village to the museum — 3 km. Local taxi (~5–7 AZN) or walk in the heat

**Downside:** the mud volcanoes are another 10–12 km off-road — no buses go there.

### Option 2: Taxi from Baku (Optimal)

Only via Bolt or Uber AZ:

- One way — ~20–25 AZN
- Round trip with waiting — negotiate 55–70 AZN. That's a fair price for 4–5 hours of the driver's time

## Mud Volcanoes: Important Updates

The old practice of loading tourists into Soviet-era Ladas for 20 AZN is gone. A proper road now leads to the new complex.

- **Entry to new complex** — ~15 AZN (includes access to equipped trails and viewpoints)
- If you want «wild» volcanoes where you can touch the mud — look for them away from the complex. But Bolt won't go there — it'll get stuck

## Day Plan

Best combination to save time:

- **09:00** — depart from Baku
- **10:00** — museum and petroglyphs (before the heat)
- **12:00** — mud volcanoes
- **13:30** — lunch at fish restaurants near Bibi-Heybat on the way back
