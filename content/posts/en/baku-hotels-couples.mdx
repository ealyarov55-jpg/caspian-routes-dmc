Baku in May 2026 is a city in full bloom. But the romance will quickly fade if you book a hotel with a «courtyard view» or in a neighborhood without walkable areas. Here's where to find the right atmosphere.

---

## What Matters for Couples in 2026

- **Location is everything:** Baku traffic has gotten worse. Choose a hotel where the Boulevard, Torgovaya Street and Icherisheher are within walking distance
- **Visual capital:** a Caspian or Flame Towers view is worth the premium. At night the tower illumination is a free show right in your window
- **Breakfast:** look for hotels offering local products — Gabala honey, Motal cheese, Zira tomatoes

---

## Budget Segment ($60–100 per night)

Forget $30. In 2026 that only gets you a hostel in the center.

### Boutique Hotels in Icherisheher

The Old City is ideal for those who want to wake up in silence among stone walls.

- **Pros:** authentic carpets, rooftop terraces, atmosphere
- **Minus:** narrow streets. Bolt stops at the fortress gates — you walk with your luggage from there

## Mid-Range ($100–200 per night)

### Fountain Square Area (Torgovaya)

The center of life. Step out of the hotel and you're immediately in a flow of restaurants and lights. In 2026 the trend is modern apart-hotels — more space than a standard room with stylish design.

### First-Line Boulevard Hotels

Here you pay for the ability to step out for a morning walk along the sea in 1 minute.
