Winter in Baku (December–February) is +5–10°C, which feels like -5°C due to humidity and wind. But it's the best time for those who hate queues and love an empty city.

---

## Winter 2026 Overview

- **Wind is the main enemy:** Baku is a city of winds. Winter wind cuts to the bone — a regular coat won't save you, you need a windproof jacket
- **Low season:** hotels are genuinely 30% cheaper, but top restaurants are still packed with locals on weekends
- **Daylight:** it gets dark around 17:30 — plan all outdoor walks for the morning

---

## Warming Itinerary

### Icherisheher

In winter the Old City becomes melancholic — wet cobblestones, sea fog, almost no tourists.

- **Maiden Tower** — 15 AZN
- **Palace of the Shirvanshahs** — 15 AZN
- Step into any artist's workshop — it's warm, smells of paint, and you might get free tea if a conversation starts

### Museum Cluster

Winter is perfect for museums — powerful heating inside:

- **Heydar Aliyev Center** — 15 AZN. A must for the architecture
- **Carpet Museum** — 15 AZN. Ideal climate inside
- **History Museum** — 10–15 AZN. In the former Taghiyev mansion — interiors impress more than exhibits

## Yanardag and Ateshgah

In winter the «Burning Mountain» looks most dramatic — the warmth from the ground is genuinely felt when it's cold around.

- **Combined ticket:** 15 AZN (both sites)
- **Transport:** Bolt taxi from center — around 10–12 AZN one way

## Winter Menu: Caloric Bombs

- **Khash** — simmered overnight. Eaten strictly in the morning (07:00–09:00) with garlic and vodka. This is a ritual
- **Piti** — the most winter soup from Sheki. Clay pot, lamb, chickpeas and fat tail. Price: 12–18 AZN
- **Düşbərə** — tiny dumplings in rich broth with dried mint. Perfect after a walk on the windy boulevard

---

## Shahdag and Tufandag (Skiing)

If Baku gets boring — head to the mountains (3 hours away).

- **Transport:** buses from «Koroglu» metro or taxi (~100 AZN per car)
- **Day ski pass 2026:** around 45–55 AZN
- **Shahdag** — more modern and expensive
- **Tufandag** — closer to Gabala city, more affordable
