Updated: May 13, 2026 · Safety Level: High ✅

Azerbaijan remains one of the safest destinations in the region for solo travelers and families. But there are nuances every traveler should know before arriving.

---

## General Crime Rate

Street crime like mugging or theft is extremely rare. You can safely walk through Baku Boulevard or the Old City at 2 AM without concern.

- **Police:** High presence in tourist areas. Generally helpful, often speak Russian or basic English
- **Solo female travelers:** Very safe in Baku. When visiting rural regions, modest dress covering shoulders and knees is recommended to respect local customs

## Common Scams

The one area where you need to stay alert.

- **Taxis:** The most common issue. Street taxis charge 3–5x the actual fare. Fix: use Bolt or Uber AZ only. A typical city ride costs 4–8 AZN
- **Bar overcharging:** Some bars in the city center may add extra items to your bill. Fix: always ask for a menu with prices before ordering

## Traffic & Road Safety

Statistically the biggest risk in Azerbaijan.

- **Driving style:** Aggressive. Pedestrians aren't always given right of way, even at crosswalks. Always double-check before stepping onto the road
- **Pedestrian underpasses:** Baku has an extensive network of underground crossings — use them instead of crossing main roads

---

## Borders & Restricted Areas

- **Karabakh:** Visiting newly liberated territories (Shusha, Aghdam, etc.) requires special government permits or joining an official tour group
- **Land borders:** As of May 2026, all land borders remain closed. Entry and exit only by air
