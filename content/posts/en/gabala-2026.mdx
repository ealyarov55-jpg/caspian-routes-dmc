Gabala is the Caucasian Switzerland. No extreme mountaineering — but top infrastructure, cable cars, mountain lakes and dense forests. Perfect for family comfort or a mountain recharge.

## Getting There from Baku

**Stadler Train** — most comfortable option. Travel time 3.5 hours, price 15–25 AZN one way.

**Bus** — from Baku International Bus Terminal. Travel time ~4 hours, price 11–13 AZN.

**Taxi** — Bolt/Uber one way 70–90 AZN. Shared minivan from bus terminal — 15–20 AZN per person.

## What to Do and How Much It Costs

**Tufandag Mountain Resort** — the main attraction. Four cable car lines reaching 1,660 meters. In summer: giant swings over the cliff, quad bikes and hiking trails. Full cable car pass — 30 AZN.

**Nohur Lake** — beautiful mountain lake at ~700m altitude. Free entry, paddleboat rental — 15–20 AZN for 20 minutes.

**Seven Beauties Waterfall** — located in Vandam village, 12 km from Gabala. Seven cascades with wooden stairs to the top. Entry 5 AZN, taxi from center — 10 AZN.

**Gabaland** — amusement park for kids and teenagers. Entry 5 AZN, rides 3–7 AZN each.

## What to Try

**Dash-arasi kebab** — meat grilled between two heated mountain stones. Smoky, unique flavour. Price 18–25 AZN per portion.
