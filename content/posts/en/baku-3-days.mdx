## Day 1: Old City and Caspian Boulevard

### Morning — Icherisheher

Start with the Old City — best visited in the morning before tourist groups arrive. Enter through the Shamakhi Gate and wander without a fixed route: narrow streets, stone houses, the smell of spices from small shops.

**Must-see inside:**
- **Maiden Tower** — symbol of Baku. Climb to the top for views of the Caspian. Entry: 15 AZN for foreigners. Open daily 09:00–18:00
- **Palace of the Shirvanshahs** — 15th century medieval complex. Entry: 15 AZN
- **Museum of Miniature Books** — free. Closed Mondays and Thursdays

Time: 2–3 hours.

**Breakfast nearby:**
Qutabs at any tea house near the Old City — thin flatbreads with meat or herbs, 2–4 AZN each.

### Afternoon — Boulevard and Flame Towers

5 minutes walk from Icherisheher to the **Seaside Boulevard** — 25 km of seafront along the Caspian. Rent a bicycle or scooter: 7–10 AZN/hour.

The **Flame Towers** — three skyscrapers shaped like flames — light up at night. A sight worth seeing.

### Evening — Where to Eat

- **Budget (20–25 AZN/person):** «Qədim Baki» or «Anadolu»
- **Mid-range (45–70 AZN):** «Firuze», «Nergiz»

**Must try:** Shah-plov, qutabs with greens and camel meat, düşbərə.

## Day 2: Fire and Stone

### Gobustan (Morning)

- **Reserve:** 15 AZN (museum + petroglyphs)
- **Mud volcanoes:** taxi drivers near the museum will take you for 20–25 AZN per car. A Lada is the only real transport there

### Ateshgah and Yanardag

- **Combined ticket:** 15 AZN (better value than buying separately at 9 AZN each)
- **Yanardag:** visit strictly after sunset — the daytime effect is disappointing

## Day 3: Modern Baku and Shopping

### Highland Park

Take the funicular (1 AZN — often under maintenance, use Bolt taxi ~3 AZN) or walk up. Best panorama of Baku — especially at sunset when the Flame Towers start glowing.

### Heydar Aliyev Center

Designed by Zaha Hadid. Entry: 15 AZN. Inside: classic cars and models of Azerbaijan. The building exterior is free and unmissable for photos.

### Shopping

- **Deniz Mall** — most modern shopping center on the boulevard
- **Souvenirs in Icherisheher** — bargain hard, knock at least 30% off

## Practical Info — May 2026

**Transport:** metro and bus — 0.50 AZN. BakiKart required (card itself 2 AZN). Taxi only via apps — Bolt, Uber AZ. City center ride: 3–6 AZN.

**Money:** Russian bank cards don't work since 2022. Bring cash (USD/EUR/RUB) and exchange at official exchange offices.

**SIM card:** airport SIM cards from 25 AZN. In the city (Azercell/Bakcell) — 15–20 AZN.
