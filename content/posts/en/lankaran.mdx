Lankaran is Azerbaijan's Batumi: tea plantations, citrus orchards, therapeutic black volcanic sand, and a humid subtropical climate. Travelers come here for a completely different landscape and a unique culinary identity.

---

## Getting There (2026 Logistics)

- **Train:** From Baku Central Railway Station. Travel time is 5–6 hours (old track, trains run slow). Price: 7–15 AZN for a compartment. Great overnight option
- **Bus / Minibus:** From Baku International Bus Terminal (m. Avtovagzal). Travel time ~4.5 hours. Price: 10–12 AZN
- **Shared Taxi:** A seat from m. 20 Yanvar or Bus Terminal costs 15–20 AZN per person. Private ride via Bolt/Uber: 100–130 AZN

## What to See

### Lankaran Fortress & Zindan

The historic Round Tower — a medieval fortress dungeon where Stalin was once imprisoned. Free to view from the outside.

### Tea Plantations

Lankaran is the tea capital of Azerbaijan. Tours to rural plantations (like Khanbulan) including tasting: 15–25 AZN.

### Gizilagaj National Park

A massive wetland reserve. Peak season for flamingos, pelicans and herons: October to March. Entry for foreigners: 2 AZN.

### Black Sand Beaches

Volcanic, dark grey, iodine-rich sand. Public beaches are free to enter.

## Food: The Legendary Southern Flavors

Southern Azerbaijani cuisine is vastly different from Baku's. The king here is **Levengi** — a rich stuffing made from crushed walnuts, onions and sour plum paste.

- **Chicken or Fish Levengi** — local Kutum fish or chicken stuffed with Levengi and slow-baked in a clay tandir. 15–30 AZN
- **Tiro Plov** — unique local layered saffron rice with herbs and smoked Caspian fish
- **Local Tea** — large-leaf Buket tea in armudu glass. Set with lemon and feijoa or walnut preserves: 5–10 AZN

## When to Visit

- **Spring (April–May):** Ideal. Everything blooming, lemon-scented air, +18–23°C
- **Autumn (September–October):** Feijoa and mandarin harvest, migratory birds at Gizilagaj
- **Summer (June–August):** Worst choice. Subtropical humidity — +35°C feels like +45°C
